# Product Naming Brainstorm

## Priority
Medium

## Description
Brainstorm and decide on a product name for "X by Breez Technologies". The product is a multi-agent workspace / AI team manager — a control room where you assemble AI experts, assign them to projects, and monitor their work.

## Candidates

### Current
- **The Agency** — staffing agency metaphor, "agency" = capacity to act, already fits the expert/team model

### Mission / Command
- **Mission** — NASA energy, natural language ("launch a mission"), works as CLI
- **Mission Control** — instantly understood, rich marketing language (launch, briefing, ground control)
- **Control** — short, bold, but generic and hard to own in search
- **Helm** — captain's wheel, one syllable, also a real named wind in England
- **Bridge** — Star Trek command deck
- **Tower** — air traffic control
- **Deck** — command deck
- **Ops** — operations center
- **Base** — home base / mission base
- **Post** — command post

### Action / Team
- **Assemble** — Avengers energy, "assemble your AI team", verb with momentum
- **Rally** — assemble the team, send them out
- **Launch** — you launch agents on missions
- **Dispatch** — sending agents out on jobs
- **Forge** — where things get built

### Weather / Wind (Breez brand alignment)
- **Gale** — powerful wind, force multiplier
- **Gust** — quick, energetic
- **Tempest** — dramatic, orchestrated chaos
- **Zephyr** — gentle west wind, elegant
- **Trades** — trade winds + skilled tradespeople (double meaning)
- **Drift** — wind drift, agents drifting across projects
- **Surge** — storm surge, power scaling up
- **Front** — weather front + operational front
- **Vane** — weathervane, you set the direction
- **Thermal** — rising warm air that lifts everything
- **Nimbus** — rain cloud + aura of power
- **Loft** — where warm air goes, elevated workspace
- **Swell** — building momentum
- **Updraft** — rising, lifting everything up
- **Slipstream** — effortless speed

### Named Winds
- **Mistral** — famous cold north wind (note: AI company conflict)
- **Chinook** — warm dry wind + heavy-lift helicopter
- **Derecho** — straight-line windstorm, Spanish for "straight/right"
- **Sirocco** — hot Mediterranean wind
- **Haboob** — massive dust storm wall, unforgettable
- **Bora** — fierce cold wind, four letters

### Sky / Sea
- **Cirrus** — high fast-moving clouds
- **Stratos** — stratosphere
- **Alto** — high, musical
- **Zenith** — the point directly above
- **Apex** — highest point
- **Crest** — wave crest
- **Keel** — keeps everything on course
- **Mast** — the tall center
- **Reef** — manage your power output

### Wind in Other Languages
- **Umoya** — Zulu (wind + spirit/soul)
- **Wayra** — Quechua (wind spirit)
- **Kaze** (風) — Japanese
- **Aura** — Latin for breeze (before it meant "vibe")
- **Pavan** — Hindi (wind + a slow stately dance)
- **Vata** — Sanskrit (wind + life force in Ayurveda)
- **Vent** — French (also English: air vent)
- **Angin** — Malay/Indonesian
- **Anem** — Greek root (anemometer)

### Misc / Sci-Fi
- **Nexus** — connection point
- **Cortex** — brain's executive control center
- **Beacon** — central point agents orient around
- **Signal** — you send signals, agents respond
- **Pulse** — heartbeat of your operation
- **Flare** — signal that kicks things off
